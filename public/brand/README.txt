The default brand mark is rose.png: the supplied dew-kissed crimson rose on a
black OLED-style background. It is bundled locally and makes no external image
requests. rose.svg is retained only as a lightweight legacy/fallback asset.

The shortcuts directory contains local SVG marks sourced from Simple Icons.
Its CC0 license is included as shortcuts/simple-icons-LICENSE.md. Music keeps
only the locally bundled SoundCloud mark. Obsolete movie and music provider
marks are intentionally removed by the installer.
